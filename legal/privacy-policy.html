This Privacy Policy explains how BaloWaci LLC ("BaloWaci," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the BaloWaci website, mobile application, feedback forms, time interpretation tools, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

This document is intended to be clear and practical. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before BaloWaci relies on it as a final legal policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We may collect information you choose to provide, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and other contact information.
  • Feedback, survey responses, product interest, support requests, and messages.
  • Business, partnership, investor, pilot, or licensing inquiries.
  • Account information if account features are added in the future.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Device and browser information, including operating system, browser type, screen size, language, and app version.
  • Usage information, including pages viewed, features used, referring pages, timestamps, and interaction events.
  • Approximate location information inferred from IP address or device settings.
  • Technical information such as IP address, network logs, crash reports, and diagnostic data.
  • Cookies, local storage, and similar technologies described in our Cookie Policy.

1.3 Location, Time, and Weather Features

BaloWaci may use time zone, location, weather, or rhythm-related information to provide contextual time interpretations. Unless a feature clearly requests precise location permission, we aim to use approximate or user-selected location information where practical.

1.4 Information From Third Parties

We may receive limited information from service providers, analytics providers, weather or time data providers, app stores, hosting platforms, and communication tools when they help us operate the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.
  • Display time, weather, rhythm, cultural, and product experience features.
  • Respond to feedback, support requests, and business inquiries.
  • Understand how people use the Service and improve design, reliability, and performance.
  • Communicate important updates, security notices, and service messages.
  • Evaluate product interest, partnerships, licensing, pilots, and investor inquiries.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information for money. We may share information:

  • With service providers that host, analyze, secure, or support the Service.
  • With professional advisers, such as attorneys, accountants, and consultants.
  • With app stores, payment providers, or platform partners if paid features are added.
  • With authorities or other parties when required by law or needed to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

If BaloWaci later uses advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising technologies, this policy should be updated and any legally required opt-out controls should be added.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies to operate the Service, remember preferences, measure usage, diagnose issues, and improve performance. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide the Service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with law. Retention periods may vary based on the type of information and the context in which it was collected.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for using secure devices, networks, and passwords if account features become available.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Request a copy of certain information in portable form.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Opt out of certain sharing, sales, targeted advertising, or profiling where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, contact us at info@balowaci.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

8. California and U.S. State Privacy Notice

Some U.S. state privacy laws provide additional rights. BaloWaci is an early-stage service and may not meet every statutory threshold for those laws, but we still aim to honor reasonable privacy requests.

In the past 12 months, we may have collected the categories listed in Section 1. We use them for the purposes listed in Section 2 and share them as described in Section 3. We do not knowingly sell personal information for money. If future practices trigger a legal "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising opt-out requirement, we will update this policy and provide the required controls.

9. International Users

If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries that may not provide the same level of data protection as your home country. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

11. Third-Party Links and Services

The Service may link to websites, platforms, app stores, social media pages, or services we do not control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new "Last Updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated through the Service or by other reasonable means.
