This Cookie Policy explains how BaloWaci LLC ("BaloWaci," "we," "our," or "us") may use cookies, local storage, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies when you use the BaloWaci website, mobile application, and related services.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website or app. Similar technologies, such as local storage and SDKs, can store information or recognize a device for similar purposes.

2. How We Use These Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Operate core site and app features.
  • Remember language, display, and preference settings.
  • Understand usage patterns and improve performance.
  • Diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and secure the Service.
  • Support weather, time zone, location, or contextual features.

4. Third-Party Technologies

Third-party providers may set or read cookies or similar identifiers when they help us provide hosting, analytics, diagnostics, weather data, app distribution, security, communication, or payment features.

We do not currently describe BaloWaci as an advertising-supported service. If advertising, retargeting, or cross-context behavioral advertising is added, this policy should be updated before launch and legally required choices should be provided.

5. Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser or device settings. You may be able to block, delete, or limit cookies and similar technologies. Some browsers also provide privacy controls for tracking technologies.

If BaloWaci adds a cookie preference tool, you may use it to manage non-essential cookies where required or offered.

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Blocking cookies may affect certain features, including remembered preferences, analytics accuracy, location or time context, forms, and future account functionality.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new "Last Updated" date.
