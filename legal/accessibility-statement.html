Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

These documents are business-ready drafts and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before public reliance.

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

BaloWaci is working to make its website, app, and related experiences accessible and usable for as many people as possible.

1. Our Goal

We aim to improve accessibility across BaloWaci by considering readable text, keyboard navigation, semantic structure, color contrast, responsive layouts, form labels, and assistive technology support.

2. Current Status

BaloWaci is an evolving product. Some prototype, creative, experimental, media, or interactive features may not yet meet every accessibility goal. We intend to keep improving these areas as the product matures.

3. Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact us and include:

  • The page, feature, or app screen involved.
  • The device, browser, operating system, or assistive technology used.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • Your contact information if you want a response.

4. Contact

  • Email: info@balowaci.com
  • Subject: Accessibility Feedback
  • Website: https://balowaci.com

We will make reasonable efforts to respond and improve the experience.
