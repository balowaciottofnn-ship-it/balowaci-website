BaloWaci Trinity

About BaloWaci

BaloWaci reimagines time through multiple interpretations — standard, military, cultural, and rotational — within one unified system. We believe time should be flexible, expressive, and meaningful across different contexts and perspectives.

A Message From the Founder

Fadhili Baloci

Welcome to BaloWaci. When I first began thinking about time, I wasn't trying to build a watch. I was trying to understand something that most of us experience every day but rarely stop to question. Time connects every person on Earth, yet we interpret it in different ways. Around the world, cultures, professions, and communities use different systems to describe the same moment. Travelers convert time zones. Pilots and military personnel use the 24-hour clock. Families and cultures often describe time according to the rhythm of the day rather than the numbers on a clock. That curiosity inspired BaloWaci. Our goal is not simply to measure time. It is to help people interpret it. BaloWaci was created around the idea that one moment can be understood through multiple synchronized perspectives. By bringing together standard time, military time, cultural interpretations, and global synchronization into a unified experience, we hope to make time more intuitive, more educational, and more meaningful. Our philosophy is simple: Everything Moving Together In Time. Those words represent more than a slogan. They represent our belief that people, cultures, and places across the world are connected through a shared passage of time, even though they may express it differently. As BaloWaci grows, our vision extends beyond watches. We see opportunities to develop technologies and experiences that help people better understand global time, improve coordination across regions, support education, and inspire curiosity about the world. Every prototype you see represents another step in that journey. Whether you are a traveler, student, professional, collector, or simply someone fascinated by time, thank you for being part of the BaloWaci story. This is only the beginning. - Fadhili Baloci Founder, BaloWaci

UX Case Study

BaloWaci turns navigation into a time experience. The watch is not decoration; it is the main control, the clock, and the product story in one interface.

Challenge

Traditional interfaces separate navigation from meaning, forcing users to learn structure before understanding context. I designed BaloWaci to unify navigation, time interpretation, and storytelling into a single interactive system.

Feedback & Iteration

Initial feedback from users showed confusion around interpreting multiple time systems at once. This led me to simplify visual hierarchy and guide interaction through motion.

Decisions

Each watch title became a destination, region, and live time zone. Rotating the bezel creates a clear cause-and-effect pattern: choose a marker, see the local time, then enter the section.

Outcome

Users were able to navigate and understand the system without reading instructions first, validating the watch as both interface and storytelling mechanism.

Design Philosophy

I design systems where interaction carries meaning. Instead of separating function and story, I aim to merge them into a single intuitive experience.
